Common questions.

Do my customers need to do anything different?

No. They keep calling the same number you've always used. The forwarding happens silently on your end — they never know a service is involved unless they don't reach you and get a text back.

What if I answer the call? Will they still get a text?

No. CallLocally only kicks in when the call goes unanswered and is forwarded by your carrier. If you pick up, nothing happens on our side.

How long does it take before leads actually come through?

Forwarding works the moment you set it up — usually within 5 minutes of finishing signup. Full carrier verification on the texting number takes about a week, but leads still flow during that window at slightly reduced delivery strength.

What number do my customers see when they get texted?

A dedicated CallLocally number that's exclusively yours. It's a real local 10-digit number, not a short code — so customers can reply naturally and the thread looks like any other text conversation.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your dashboard in two clicks. No contracts, no commitments. If you cancel during the 14-day trial, you're not charged anything.

What trades does this work for?

CallLocally is built specifically for home service contractors — plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, roofers, and similar trades. If you work the trades and miss calls while on jobs, this is for you.
